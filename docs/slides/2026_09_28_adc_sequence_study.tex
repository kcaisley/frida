    \item At 10 MSPS, ADC15 mean stays about 3.2 LSB below its 2-MSPS control. All saved exact-10-MSPS C5/C6 captures use one nominal 50-mV input, so transfer accuracy and input-referred noise remain unverified.
    \item None of the eight measured 6/8 recipes combines a plausible code with sigma below 1 LSB. Longer COMP high time alone has not reduced the floor.
    \item C5/L2 to C6/L2 keeps LOGIC fixed, makes the measured COMP-low pulse shorter and less deep, and changes physical codes sharply at 10 MSPS. PEX gives nearly identical codes and complete latch precharge.
    \item Short LOGIC or extra idle removes the all-one mode. LOGIC also changes 6/8 B12 repeats in ADC07/15, which carry a shared tone. Unmeasured LVDS and clock paths leave silicon reset unverified; FRIDA-2 has no physical counterpart here.
  \end{itemize}
