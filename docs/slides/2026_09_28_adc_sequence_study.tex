\begin{frame}{The 5/8 to 6/8 pair shows a high-rate code shift}
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.98\linewidth,height=0.82\textheight,keepaspectratio]{\analysispath/physical_comp_duty_pair.pdf}
  \slidesource{Corrected 26 Sep scan; 16 ADCs, three rates, 100,000 conversions per setting}
  \note{C5/L2 to C6/L2 changes only the COMP row, retaining identical INIT, SAMP, and LOGIC rows. Median absolute mean-code shift across 16 ADCs is 0.085, 1.854, and 6.660 LSB at 320, 960, and 1600 MBd. The 6/8 low time falls to about 1.215 ns from 1.857 ns, while LOGIC-to-next-COMP stays near 1.23 ns and LOGIC moves from 0.617 ns after COMP fall to approximately coincident with it. C6/L2 to C6/L3 delays LOGIC with COMP fixed and changes the 1600-MBd median mean code by only 0.373 LSB. In physical ADC03, B11 is low in every 1600-MBd C5/L2 and C6/L2 conversion. B12 equals that preceding low bit in 385/100,000 C5/L2 conversions and 99,999/100,000 C6/L2 conversions. This is compatible with a retained preceding decision but does not prove why it was retained. The retained scope conversion agrees with FastRX on B12 for all three recipes, but samples the delayed output only once per capture. The paired result points to the COMP low/capture interval, but does not separate internal reset from LOGIC setup or clock delivery.}

\begin{frame}{Long idle between conversions does not rescue 6/8}
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.98\linewidth,height=0.82\textheight,keepaspectratio]{\analysispath/historical_idle_control.pdf}
  \slidesource{Independent 25 Sep physical scan; 192 selected HDF5 captures, 100,000 conversions each}
  \note{The historical 168- and 256-symbol rows share identical first 160 symbols, so their 5-ns decision periods and COMP/LOGIC timing match at 1600 MBd. The 256-symbol row adds 88 all-low symbols and lowers the actual repeat rate from 9.524 to 6.25 MSPS. Median absolute C6-minus-C5 mean-code shifts across 16 ADCs are 6.722 and 6.196 LSB for the two lengths, while adding idle to C6 changes the median mean code by only 0.269 LSB. ADC03 B12 remains low in all 100,000 C6 conversions for each length but high in about 98 percent of the C5 conversions. All 192 selected files have valid single-record scope comparisons, zero bit mismatches, and zero lost frames. This argues against insufficient between-conversion recovery; it does not test the internal reset between successive bit decisions. The corrected 160-symbol 10-MSPS scan remains the primary physical result.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{The archived 0.54-LSB result has a short final comparison}
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.98\linewidth,height=0.82\textheight,keepaspectratio]{\analysispath/old_half_lsb_precedent.pdf}
  \slidesource{Archived 14 Sep physical ADC03 and 6 Sep PEX; corrected 26 Sep physical and PEX}
  \note{The archived physical file stores the same 160-symbol INIT, SAMP, COMP, and LOGIC rows as the PEX file with a 48-symbol cyclic offset. Both use INIT4, SAMP24, COMP 11111100, and one-symbol LOGIC. B0--B15 receive six COMP-high symbols, but B16 begins at symbol 156 and receives only four before the next INIT. Archived PEX sigma is 0.538 LSB across 99 saved conversions; physical ADC03 sigma is 3.186 LSB across 100,000. The corrected SAMP20, four-symbol LOGIC case gives 0.703 LSB in PEX and 3.309 LSB physically; a corrected physical SAMP20, one-symbol LOGIC capture gives 3.447 LSB. PEX and physical conditions differ beyond the digital pattern. The old and new PEX setups also differ in SAMP duration, LOGIC width, and output sampling, so their code-spread difference cannot be assigned to the B16 truncation alone.}
\end{frame}

The archived 0.538-LSB code spread cannot establish a healthy 17-decision conversion, while the different old and new conditions prevent a single-cause attribution for the spread change.}

\begin{frame}{5/8 remains the best saved candidate; 0.5 LSB is unverified}
  \small
  \begin{itemize}
    \item At 1600 MBd, several 5/8 recipes have 14/16 plausible physical ADCs. The smallest full-capture sigma is 0.633 LSB on ADC15, with rare 15-LSB code jumps.
    \item None of the eight measured 6/8 recipes combines a plausible code with sigma below 1 LSB. Longer COMP high time alone has not reduced the floor.
    \item C5/L2 to C6/L2 keeps LOGIC fixed yet changes physical mean codes sharply at 10 MSPS. PEX gives nearly identical codes and complete latch precharge for both.
    \item Extra idle between conversions leaves the 6/8 failure intact. Silicon reset between bit decisions and the exact failure mechanism remain unverified; FRIDA-2 has no physical counterpart here.
  \end{itemize}
  \slidesource{29-recipe physical screen; matched FRIDA-1 PEX timing and latch analysis}
\end{frame}
