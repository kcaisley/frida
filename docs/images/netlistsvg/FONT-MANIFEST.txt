latin_modern_mono_subset.woff is a subset of lmmono10-regular.otf from the Latin Modern font family, generated from TeX Live 2026 with fontTools 4.58.3. It contains printable ASCII, Latin-1, and Delta/Omega/mu for schematic labels. The subset is embedded in the two generated SVG skins as a data URL under the family name FRIDA Latin Modern Mono.
