# ADC schematic generation

The schematic is authored in Circuitikz in [adc_architecture.tex](../../../build/summary_adc_figures/adc_architecture.tex), beside its generated PDF and PNG in `build/summary_adc_figures/`. It began from the repository's hand-drawn ADC diagram and follows the driver geometry in [daq_diagram.tex](../daq_diagram.tex). The report uses the PNG; the PDF preserves vector lines and text for publication.

From the repository root, regenerate with:

```bash
cd build/summary_adc_figures
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error adc_architecture.tex
pdftoppm -r 220 -singlefile -png adc_architecture.pdf adc_architecture
cp adc_architecture.pdf adc_architecture.png ../../docs/images/report/
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error figure_review.tex
```

Stage ordering follows [adc_conventions.md](../../adc_conventions.md). Only stages 0, 1, 2, and 15 are displayed, with effective weights 768, 512, 320, and 1 times C_u; three closely spaced dots stand for the omitted stages, with stages 3–14 labelled in the central chain. Each capacitor pair is enclosed separately, with its effective weight outside the dotted rectangle. Short main and difference labels, such as C_m2 and C_d2, sit beside the symbols so the bottom-plate connections remain visible.

Native Circuitikz `flipflop D` symbols retain the default width (1.2), pin spacing (0.6), and outline thickness. At the requested presentation size, the native clock wedge size is increased to 0.35 and both side-pin and top/bottom-pin fonts are set to small, matching all signal, supply, and capacitor labels. Their Q-bar pins are hidden; branch registers add an E input. Initialization pins are on the bottom, labelled S on the first selector and R on subsequent selectors and branch registers. The branch R labels denote functional initialization to programmed starting values, not a literal clear-to-zero operation or asynchronous reset pin. The central DFF chain represents a one-hot selector: initialization seeds its first stage and clears the others, and the logic clock shifts the enable to successive P/N decision-register pairs. The init pins on all selector and P/N driver DFFs are a functional abstraction of synchronous initialization. The implementation in `design/hdl/salogic.v` loads each driver register from its programmable dac_astate value and enables all registers while init is high; at the same logic-clock edge, dac_cycle becomes 0x0001. Subsequent edges shift this selector left while inserting zeros. The selector is therefore seeded one-hot during initialization, rather than starting all-zero. Branch initial states are programmable and need not all be zero. Earlier drawings included per-stage muxes to show initialization selection explicitly; the current drawing omits those implementation details. The final seventeenth comparison has no corresponding switched-capacitor stage and contributes to external weighted decoding.

The sampling switches use native transmission-gate symbols with a single samp control above the symbol. Native input leads are suppressed and the inversion-circle radius is zero; explicit signal and control wires provide the visible connections. Complement generation is implicit in this functional schematic. The comparator uses an enlarged differential-output amplifier symbol with an added top-edge clock-input wedge, separate analog supply connections, and a bubble on its complementary output. It abstracts the StrongARM implementation, rather than showing its transistor circuit. The driver symbols likewise abstract complementary CMOS rail drivers, with each register Q physically wired to its driver input. Comparator-data buses run above the P/N register banks, separately from the Q-to-driver connections. D-input drops share the horizontal position of the clock-bus taps. The comparator clock wedge is a symmetric downward equilateral wedge centred on its vertical clock wire, rather than tilted along the sloping body edge. Its stroke is clipped to the comparator triangle, preserving the body outline and trimming both wedge arms where they meet that edge. Selector outputs are labelled E_0, E_1, E_2, and E_15; matching labelled stubs on the branch E inputs replace the long enable wires. All three logic/init terminal pairs share the same left coordinate and vertical spacing, with logic above init.

After editing, inspect both the schematic PNG and page 1 of `figure_review.pdf`, particularly the clock wedge, supply labels, capacitor bottom-plate wiring, enable connections, and dotted omissions. Refresh `report_preview.zip` after copying presentation images; its relative paths must match `docs/report.md`.

Driver output spacing is set globally in Circuitikz, because its output anchors evaluate that key when a wire is drawn. Driver height is 1.2 cm and output-height ratio is 0.583333333, giving an offset of 0.35 cm on each side; the capacitor vertical wires use those same offsets. The comparator has its own scope with an output-height ratio of 0.3. Driver and comparator body widths account for the Circuitikz 0.7 body-to-terminal width ratio, giving equilateral triangles. Complementary-output bubbles are placed on the actual triangle edges using geometric interpolation rather than the built-in body-output anchors, which do not match these custom dimensions. Supply pins are placed clear of the output leads.

All horizontal buses end at their final required connections: initialization at stage 15, the three logic buses at the final clock tap, and comparator-data buses at the first D-input tap on the left. The top comparator output is D_out with an overbar and feeds the P register bank; the lower D_out feeds the N register bank, avoiding crossed feedback routes. Circular Circuitikz ocirc terminals identify signal inputs. Labels use comp, V_inp/V_inn, V_dd/V_ss, D_out and its overbar complement, and C_mN/C_dN.

The comparator is drawn at scale 3.3, positioned close to the array with shortened output leads, with its clock pin horizontally centred between the V_dd supply tap and complementary-output bubble on the upper edge. Its supply pins are near the input edge, above and below the input polarity markers. Filled junction dots mark only joins of at least three wire segments; two-wire bends and the final initialization, logic, data, and selector taps have no junction dots. Open circles remain at explicit input terminals.

Flip-flop pin names retain Circuitikz’s default serif LaTeX font. The diagram does not override the document family to sans-serif; signal labels use the same small serif font as the pins, and electrical quantities use upright Roman math lettering. Only the capacitor-unit notation C_u remains italic.

Symbol outline weight uses the default TikZ base stroke and Circuitikz class thickness multipliers. Do not apply a heavier global line width to the circuit: it also multiplies amplifier and flip-flop body outlines. Geometry is unchanged by this styling correction.

Block fills use the Nord palette: blue at 25% for all flip-flops, orange at 30% for capacitor drivers, green at 35% for the comparator, purple at 30% for the sampling switches, and light Nord grey (#ECEFF4) inside the dotted capacitor cells. Cell fills are drawn on the background layer so they do not cover the top-plate bus. Wires, symbol outlines, and labels retain their existing dark strokes; inversion bubbles remain white. The build directory also retains adc_architecture_monochrome.tex as the preceding version.
