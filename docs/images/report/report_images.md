# ADC report: figure sources and verification notes

This companion file records implementation and verification notes for [report.md](../../report.md). Separate handoff notes describe [schematic generation](schematic_generation.md) and [capacitor generation](capacitor_generation.md).

Implemented figure sources and their regeneration commands are retained beside the PDF, PNG, and illustrative GDS outputs in build/summary_adc_figures/. adc_architecture.tex is the Circuitikz source; render_capacitor.py creates the GDS and its consistent plan/perspective renderings; capacitor_views.tex combines them; adc_timing.tex is generated by the existing sequence renderer; prepare_layout.py produces a white-background report copy of the original layout; figure_review.tex provides a three-page figure review PDF. Presentation PNGs and PDFs are copied into docs/images/report/.

Figure 2 provenance. The schematic uses the first three and final stages in conversion order from docs/adc_conventions.md, native Circuitikz DFF and transmission-gate symbols, and complementary rail-driver symbols based on the DAQ diagram. The illustrative three-layer capacitor stack is explanatory geometry, not a claim about the fabricated prototype stack. Historical one-layer M6 and two-layer M6–M7 variants are defined in flow/adc/layout.py; confirm the selected prototype flavour before identifying foundry layers. The central DFF chain is a functional one-hot selector; The init pin denotes synchronous initialization, abstracting its implementation. Initialization loads all driver registers from their programmable A states and sets the first selector stage while clearing the others; the input muxes are intentionally omitted. Comparator supply and clock pins are shown separately, with complementary outputs. Native DFF Q-bar pins are hidden.

Draft provenance and remaining checks. The draft currently contains six image files, including the combined capacitor views; combine the survey panels and keep architecture/capacitor insets small to approach a 2–3 page Word layout. No fixed page count is implied by Markdown. The approximately 1 Mpixel (1024 × 1024) example is illustrative, rather than a committed sensor architecture; substitute the intended array, pixel pitch, fluence, gain, and power budget when agreed. The existing survey panels come from phys/adc_survey.py and have a target of 11 ENOB, 100 µW, 10 MS/s, and 3,600 µm². They use effective Nyquist rate for survey conversion energy and FoM; note that convention when comparing low-bandwidth fixed-input tests. Source survey PNGs were copied from phys/results because that generated directory is not tracked. Channel dimensions and whole-chip power come from docs/slides/2026_08_18_adc_guide.tex. The nominal branch sum of 1.8824 pF and 0.8 fF effective unit are obtained from design/spice/caparray.cdl; the January slides list an approximate 17 × 50 µm capacitor-array footprint, which should be verified against the chosen layout before publication. An 800 fF conventional 12-bit binary array does not imply a 2 aF smallest element: simple scaling gives about 0.2–0.4 fF depending on topology, so the proposed 2 aF claim is omitted. Available power plots are build/analysis/adc/20260818_0146/spice_ideal_power_vs_conversion_rate.png and spice_pex_power_vs_conversion_rate.png, with later gallery candidates under build/analysis/adc/20260819_1734/. Confirm raw inputs, extraction vintage, supply conditions, timing, and per-channel scope before transferring numbers; do not interpolate the 6 MS/s value. Fixed-input noise plots exist under build/analysis/adc/20260808_0141/, but dynamic timing issues documented in the September sequence study require scrutiny before claiming achieved 11-ENOB operation at 10 MS/s. Use flow/adc/behavioral.py and established analysis plotters for the proposed DNL/INL figure; show actual mismatch assumptions and calibrated/uncalibrated decoding. Harpe's cited JSSC paper appeared online in 2018 and in print in 2019. To regenerate the schematic, run `pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error adc_architecture.tex` from `build/summary_adc_figures/`, followed by `pdftoppm -r 220 -singlefile -png adc_architecture.pdf adc_architecture`. Copy the PDF and PNG into `docs/images/report/`. After regenerating either figure, compile `figure_review.tex` from the same directory to refresh the review PDF. The local Zed preview bundle, `build/summary_adc_figures/report_preview.zip`, packages `docs/report.md` as `report.md` with its relative `images/report/` files; recreate it after changing the report or presentation images.

The introductory fluence-rate statement follows the integrating-detector comparison in docs/slides/detectors.tex and Philipp et al. (2022), DOI 10.1017/S1431927622000174. Their detector spans single-electron sensitivity and incident currents above 180 pA per 150 × 150 µm pixel. Dividing current by the elementary charge and pixel area gives approximately 5 × 10^10 primary electrons/mm²/s, or 5 × 10^12/cm²/s; the report conservatively states above 10^12/cm²/s. This is an incident primary-electron rate, not secondary signal charge, and the area units were checked against the publication.
