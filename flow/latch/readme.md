| Testbench generator | ✓ | [sim.py](sim.py) | One bench for every case (bag3_digital's sequential timing benches): complementary clocks, data toggling once per cycle a set offset before each capturing edge (a flip-flop's rising, a latch's closing edge), an initial reset, set and reset pulses, output loads. The upset bench (FE-I4's and RD53's SEU benches): the latch takes a 1 for a nanosecond and holds it; a double-exponential current (5 ps rise, 50 ps fall, a Spectre literal on the hierarchical node) puts a charge on the storage node then holding a 0, which carries no load in either latch. |
| Simulation runners | ✓ | [sim.py](sim.py) | `latch_characterization`: in each PDK about its own core supply, every case at 75–108 % of it; at the core supply the latches and flip-flops over data offsets −200 to 300 ps, the flip-flops and dividers over clock periods 2 ns to 30 ps, the C2MOS and DICE latches struck with 1 to 200 fC; 372 transients per PDK, all 2232 in about eight minutes on 16 workers; `check=True` runs Spectre's static checks at the nominal point. |
| Analysis | ✓ | [analysis.py](analysis.py) | `MeasLatch`; `analyze_latch_timing` (launch-to-q delays, edges taking the new or keeping the old value, energy per cycle), `analyze_latch_constraint` (setup at a 10 % delay pushout, hold), `analyze_latch_sr` (set and reset delays), `analyze_latch_upset` (whether a strike flips the held 1, q's dip, the critical charge). In TSMC65 at 1.2 V: latch setup 60 ps and hold −40 ps, flip-flop 50 ps and −30 ps; the divider toggles to 10 GHz (TSMC28 16.7 GHz, the 130–180 nm PDKs 3.3–5 GHz, where setup reaches 90–150 ps). Run 20261019_234509: the TSPC divider toggles 1.5–3 times faster than the C2MOS one (TSMC65 16.7 GHz, IHP130 10 against 3.3 GHz) and its flip-flop sets up in 10–70 ps with 10–30 ps of hold; the C2MOS latch flips at 7 fC in TSMC28 to 50 fC in LF150 and Tower180, the DICE latch keeps its bit at 200 fC in every PDK, its q dipping fully and recovering, at 2.8–4.4 times the C2MOS latch's energy per cycle. With write gates sharing d's complement, a strike on one node reached its partner through them (TSMC28, 50 fC): hence a writer per node. |
