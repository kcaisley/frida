| Assembler | ✓ | [static/js/sweep.js](static/js/sweep.js) | The block's sweep, or one of its optimizations' studies (the Search above the table, as the layout's Generator: `sweep` or a problem as `adc(bits=12)`, from the catalog's `searches`, `flow.opt.runner.table`), as one table on its side: a row per field, the PDK first (its dot in its colour and its marker in the scatter), then the parameters that differ between the instances, then the metrics at a row's SI prefix; a column per instance, scrolled sideways (only the columns in view are drawn), the labels and heads staying put. A row's label sorts the instances by it either way and filters them: tick the values to keep ("only" keeps one), or bound a numeric row; the table's corner shows how many pass (its run on hover), the bar clears the filters. Beside it a scatter plot of any metric or numeric parameter against any other (log axes offered for positive values, chosen for those spanning two decades), a point coloured and shaped by its PDK, on `flow/analysis/plots.py`'s paper, its legend under it; zoom or pan, fit and PNG (with the legend) in its toolbar, each a button with its icon and name as the bar's, wheel zoom. Hovering a column rings its point; hovering a point lights its column and scrolls to it. Clicking a column's ○ or a point marks the instance, ringed in the plot: marking loads nothing. View (one marked) and Compare (two or more) open the marked instances in place; All instances (or Back) returns here with them still marked, their panels unloaded. Sort, filters, axes and marks persist per table in the browser; on a first visit a table's own sort and axes (`sort`, `axes`: a study's, by cost and cost against trial), else energy against delay. A row a table dims (`dim`: a study's design that missed a bound, at the cost of what was measured) is greyed in the table, sorted after the rest and drawn grey under them; the plot opens with its metric axes on the rest (their SI prefixes too), Fit or a double-click shows all. A row with an x and no y is a tick in a strip over the plot, a lane by the table's `rug` field (a study's designs rejected before they had a cost, by their stage); a table's `bands` shade a range of an axis it plots, labelled (a study's warm start over its trials). An instance is its PDK and parameters: its id names it only in the URL. |
