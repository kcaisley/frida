# Status

| Feature | Status | File | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data | ✓ | [../sweep/runner.py](../sweep/runner.py) | `python -m flow web.tables` (as `python -m flow sweep.tables`): each block's newest sweep (`build/sweep/<block>/<run>/`) as one table (`build/web/data/sweep/<block>.json`): a row per instance with its PDK, topology and sizing, the parameters that differ between instances, and every metric; each condition sweep's axis limits over all instances. And a file per instance (its parameters, metrics, condition sweeps, waves, layout and error), its ALIGN layout flattened onto the PDK's generic layers ([export.py](export.py)), its schematic and netlist, and the index of its waveform measurements. Nothing is simulated or laid out. |
| Server | ✓ | [server.py](server.py) | `serve` (`python -m flow web.serve`, port 8765) answers `/` from `static/`, `/data/` from `build/web/data`, `/api/wave` with one record of a cached measurement's waveforms, read from its HDF5 file under `build/` when asked for (every k-th sample past 20 000), `/api/gds` with a layout's GDS as a download, and the layout compiler's `/api/compile/` ([jobs.py](jobs.py)) with its jobs' files under `/data/compile/`. |
| Assembler | ✓ | [static/index.html](static/index.html), [static/js/explorer.js](static/js/explorer.js) | The page "Assembler", in GoldenLayout tabs as the instance page (Blocks, Sweeps, Metrics), every toolbar a dropdown labelled on its left or icon buttons: the blocks with a sweep, by their folder under `flow/` with the instance count in grey (as Zed's git panel); the chosen block's instances in one table (pinned to its tools column: pin, Open) under its title, the count shown and Clear filters, its columns grouped as the instance (PDK, and the sweep's topology and sizing names), the parameters those set and the metrics. A column header sorts either way and filters: tick the values to keep (PDK, topology, sizing, categorical parameters; "only" keeps one), or bound a numeric column. Beside it a scatter plot of any metric or numeric parameter against any other (log axes offered for positive columns, chosen for those spanning two decades), coloured by PDK in `CURVE_COLORS` order and marked by topology, on `flow/analysis/plots.py`'s paper, its legend under it; zoom or pan, fit and PNG (with the legend) in its toolbar, wheel zoom. Hovering a row rings its point; hovering a point lights its row and scrolls to it; clicking a point or a row's pin pins it, ringed and labelled, across axis changes and reloads. Filters, sort, axes and pins persist per block in the browser. |
| Explorer | ✓ | [static/detail.html](static/detail.html), [static/js/detail.js](static/js/detail.js) | The instance page, `detail.html?block=<block>&id=<pdk>__<topology>__<sizing>`, opened from an Assembler row: in a tab of its own: GoldenLayout panels for its summary (one table: its parameters, metrics with SI units, the ALIGN layout's size and signoff; the error of a failed instance), its condition sweeps (Analyses), each plotted on its own data: curves (several a colour each, their legend under the plot, never over it), the ADC's decision paths weighted by how many conversions took each, or maps (a heatmap a detail), and the shared views below on the instance alone. |
| Compiler | ✓ | [static/compile.html](static/compile.html), [static/js/compile.js](static/js/compile.js), [jobs.py](jobs.py), [../layout/lint.py](../layout/lint.py), [../layout/compile.py](../layout/compile.py) | The page "Compiler" (linked from the Assembler): a netlist laid out with open tools only. Flow (top left: PDK, style Symmetric or Dense, Run and Cancel, the stages as chips with their times, History of runs, GDS and log), the netlist in CodeMirror under it (one `.subckt` of the PDK's row transistors and library cells, checked 0.5 s after each edit: lint marks by line), Diagnostics under that (the netlist's, layout's, DRC and LVS statistics; the issues by source, a netlist one jumps to its line, a DRC one to its markers); the layout, 3D and layers top right and the schematic bottom right, as on the instance page. A run is a job (`build/compile/<id>/`, at most four at a time): the check and schematic, ALIGN on its own (its log followed into constraints, primitives, placement and routing; another outline, then ALIGN's default router, where the PDK's fails), gdscheck (its own full deck of an open PDK, about a second; else the repo's precheck), the KLayout LVS deck where it has one (the approximate one in TSMC65), and the viewer's layout. `?job=<id>` reopens a run. |
| Comparer | ✓ | [static/js/detail.js](static/js/detail.js) | `detail.html?block=<block>&ids=<id>,<id>,...`, from the Assembler's Compare button on its pinned instances: their parameters (those that differ, or all) and metrics in one table, a column each under its colour, and their analyses overlaid an instance a colour (maps side by side) under one key to the colours (an instance clicked in it hides or shows its curves in every plot), a detail's several series a dash each, keyed under its plot; no layout, schematic or waveforms. |
| Waveforms | ✓ | [static/js/traces.js](static/js/traces.js) | The instance's transient runs, after Surfer's waveform viewer: every net and supply current a row on one time axis, its name, range and controls beside it (hidden rows back from +), analog or digital (mid-swing threshold); wheel zoom, drag pan, right-drag span, a cursor reading every row beside its name, the time under the mouse and a marker for Δt in the toolbar. |
| Layout | ✓ | [static/js/layout2d.js](static/js/layout2d.js) | Canvas of the merged layers, the front end under the metals, translucent fills with a screen-fixed hatch; pan, zoom about the cursor, zoom to a box (right-drag), fit (F), ruler (K, as KLayout), pin labels (L), the shape under the cursor and its size, its coordinates in the corner; the overlays (scale bar, pin labels, ruler) in the theme's colors; DRC markers (outlined, zoomed to from Diagnostics) and the GDS download where the layout has one. |
| 3D | ✓ | [static/js/layout3d.js](static/js/layout3d.js) | three.js, each layer extruded between nominal heights (an even stack, the same for every PDK), adjustable vertical scale, orbit, PNG export. Not checked by eye here: headless Chromium on this machine draws no WebGL geometry. |
