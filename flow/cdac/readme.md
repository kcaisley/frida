# Status

The CDAC is the capacitor array with its drivers: the array's capacitors cannot be characterized for speed without the drivers that switch them. [flow/caparray](../caparray/readme.md) stays the array's generator (weights, layouts, extractions) and [flow/capdriver](../capdriver/readme.md) the drivers' (cells, strengths, and the drives an array's plates call for); this block puts them together and measures how each stage settles. It is on the [pipeline](../../docs/pipeline.md): `params.py` < `subckt.py` < `sim.py` < `types.py` < `analysis.py` < `plots.py` < `sweep.py`, each importing only those before it, and `model.py` above them.

| Feature | Status | File | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parameters | ✓ | [params.py](params.py) | `Params`: the CDAC (`dac`: each side's main and difference drivers, `CapDriverParams`, and its plates, `main_f`, `diff_f`, `parasitic_f`) and the converter resolution its settling is measured to (`adc_bits`); `valid` (a main and a difference plate for each stage, none negative). `TbParams`, one testbench run: the PDK, the design, the supply (the DAC's reference), the window each stage switches in, Spectre's output step; its full scale, LSB, ideal steps and each stage's window (`window_start_s`). The testbench's constants: the top plates' bleed (`BLEED_OHM`), the states' edges, Spectre's step a 2000th of the window (`SAMPLES_PER_WINDOW`) and its tolerances. |
| Netlist generator | ✓ | [subckt.py](subckt.py) | `netlist(Params)` and its nets `Nets` (the states and bottom plates a bit per stage): both sides, each with a main and a difference `CapDriver` of the array's stages (wired as `flow/adc/subckt.py` wires them: the main drivers' invert input `dac_invert`, the difference drivers' `dac_diffcaps`), its plates ideal capacitors from each bottom plate to the top plate, and the top plate's other load to ground. |
| Netlist tests | ✓ | [test_subckt.py](test_subckt.py) | The drivers' and plates' wiring, missing difference plates, the buses' widths and the nets' order, refused capacitances. |
| Testbench generator | ✓ | [sim.py](sim.py) | `testbench(TbParams)`: every stage switches alone in its turn, as the SAR loop switches it: its P side's state rises and its N side's falls a window into its turn, and back the window after, every other stage held; the top plates bled to mid-supply through 1 TΩ. `simulate(tb, run_dir)`: one Spectre transient left on disk in nutbin, the nets `Nets` marks to save (both top plates, the P side's states and main bottom plates), strobed at a 2000th of the window with the step bounded to it, reltol 1e-4: tighter tolerances (1e-5, 1e-6 conservative) move no settling time by more than 1 ps in a 10- and a 14-bit array, and the steps agree with the capacitances' within 0.06 LSB. `flow.pipeline.read` reads it as a measurement, nothing resampled. |
| Testbench tests | ✓ | [test_sim.py](test_sim.py) | Each stage's window and pulses, the saved nets in the netlist, a window too short refused. |
| Analysis | ✓ | [analysis.py](analysis.py), [types.py](types.py) | `analyze_settling` (`AnalysisSettling`): per stage and direction, on the raw time points, the differential top plate's step, the driver's delay from the state's edge to its main bottom plate's 50 % (`calc.delay`), and the settling time from that edge until the step stays within half the converter's LSB, 2 · full scale / 2^adc_bits (`calc.settlingTime`). The behavioral time constant of a stage is (settling − delay) / ln(\|step\| / ½ LSB) in its slower direction (`tau_s`): started at its driver's delay, its exponential reaches half an LSB when SPICE does. `metrics`: the slowest and the MSB's settling, the largest delay and time constant, the full scale, the LSB, the window, the steps' error against the capacitances (the simulator's precision). |
| Plots | ✓ | [plots.py](plots.py) | `plot_settling_stage` and `plot_model_stage`: the settling and the model's delay and time constant by stage, the record's plots, which `model.py` reads back. |
| Analysis tests | ✓ | [test_analysis.py](test_analysis.py) | Synthetic RC steps of known delay, time constant and amplitude on the simulator's time axis: the steps, delays, settling and time constants recovered; an unsettled window detected and refused by the record; the record's metrics and plots. |
| Design space | ✓ | [sweep.py](sweep.py) | `adc_array(pdk, array)` (`@family`): the array of every converter of `flow.adc.sweep`, at its converter's resolution, `n_dac + 1`: every ADC array the design space uses (`flow.caparray.layout.ADC_ARRAYS`, sized through its extraction in TSMC65: binary and the three schemes with 2, 4 and 6 extra cycles at 10, 12 and 14 bits, at one and two of the resolution's least switched capacitance, and FRIDA-1's array), its plates as `CapArrayModel.of` gives them, its drivers the generic cells derived from them (`flow.capdriver.model.driver_strengths`: each stage's, main and difference alike, the weakest drive, an XOR, a buffer of 2 to 16 after it or k buffers of 16 in parallel up to 256, whose RC estimate settles it within 1 ns); 61 instances in TSMC65, each its parameters. `runs`: one run, in a window twice the slowest stage's estimated settling (from the drivers' characterization, `flow.capdriver.model`), at least 1 ns; `record` raises where a stage does not settle in its window's first half (none of the 514 characterizations so far needed a longer window). |
| Simulation runner | ✓ | [sweep.py](sweep.py) | `python -m flow run cdac --workers 16`: the sweep runner (`flow.sweep.runner`), each instance's run kept as `settling/result.h5` for the viewer (every saved net). 2026-10-25, run 20261025_005807 on extraction 20261025_004530, the 14-bit arrays split at 64 with exact unary rows: all 61 on 16 Spectre runs in 71 min, a few minutes each at 10 and 12 bits, up to 52 minutes at 14 bits x2; the derived drives are 1–2 at 10 bits, up to 4 (x1) and 8 (x2) at 12, and for the 14-bit MSB 48–64 (x1) and 80–128 (x2), three to eight buffers of 16; every stage of every array settles to half an LSB in 0.06–0.75 ns, the slowest stage of an array in 0.37–0.75 ns, against 0.1 ns to 23 ns with the fixed drives before. Its records (each instance is its parameters: the pipeline's family reaches the same 61 digests) and their waveforms, retagged for the pipeline's types, are what the model and the viewer read; two 10-bit instances re-run on the pipeline give the same netlist and the same metrics to 1e-14. |
| Behavioral model | ✓ | [model.py](model.py), [test_model.py](test_model.py) | `cdac.model.Model.of(pdk, cdac.Params)`: per stage its settling, its driver's delay and its time constant, from the sweep's newest run or the build's model cache, simulated on first use (`simulate=True`). `flow.adc.model.AdcModel` settles each stage by the model of its array at its resolution (`cdac.adc_array`; `stage_tau_s`, `stage_delay_s`; `tau_scale` scales the time constants, not the delays); it only looks the characterization up (`simulate=False`, without waiting on any lock) and falls back to the drivers' RC estimate, with a warning, where there is none, so run the sweep first. |
| Layout | ✗ | - | Not a layout block: the array is laid out and extracted by flow/caparray, the drivers by flow/capdriver. |
