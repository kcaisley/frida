# Status

The CDAC is the capacitor array with its drivers: the array's capacitors cannot be characterized for speed without the drivers that switch them. [flow/caparray](../caparray/readme.md) stays the array's generator (weights, layouts, extractions) and [flow/capdriver](../capdriver/readme.md) the drivers' (cells, strengths); this block puts them together and measures how each stage settles.

| Feature | Status | File | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Netlist generator | ✓ | [subckt.py](subckt.py) | `Cdac(CdacParams)`: both sides, each with a main and a difference `CapDriver` of the array's stages (wired as `flow/adc/subckt.py` wires them: the main drivers' invert input `dac_invert`, the difference drivers' `dac_diffcaps`), its plates ideal capacitors from each bottom plate to the top plate, and the top plate's other load to ground. `cdac_params(pdk, CapArrayConfig)` takes the plates from `CapArrayModel.of`: the ADC array's 3D extraction at its unit capacitance. The drivers are the generic cells the ADC model stands in with (`Cells.GENERIC`), derived from the array's plates (`driver_strengths`, `derive_strengths`): each stage's, main and difference alike, the weakest drive (an XOR, a buffer of 2 to 16 after it, or k buffers of 16 in parallel after a pre-driver, up to 256) whose RC estimate (`settling_estimate_s`: its intrinsic delay, and its resistance through the plate in series with the rest of the top plate over the log of the plate's step over half an LSB) settles in `SETTLING_TARGET_S`, 1 ns; so a stage's drive scales with its plate and with the converter's resolution. Only FRIDA-1's fabricated arrays set `CapArrayConfig.driver_strengths`. |
| Netlist tests | ✓ | [test_subckt.py](test_subckt.py) | The drivers' and plates' wiring, missing difference plates, refused capacitances; the testbench's timing and saved nets; the derived drives (each the weakest that settles its stage in the target, larger for a larger plate, FRIDA-1's bands kept). |
| Testbench generator | ✓ | [sim.py](sim.py) | Every stage switches alone in its turn, as the SAR loop switches it: its P side's state rises and its N side's falls at the start of its window, and back at the start of the next, every other stage held; the top plates bled to mid-supply through 1 TΩ. The window is twice the slowest stage's estimated settling (from the drivers' characterization, `flow.capdriver.model`) and is doubled until every step settles in its first half. Spectre at a fixed step of a 2000th of the window and reltol 1e-4: tighter tolerances (1e-5, 1e-6 conservative) move no settling time by more than 1 ps in a 10- and a 14-bit array, and the steps agree with the capacitances' within 0.06 LSB (0.03 in the earlier ADC arrays). |
| Analysis | ✓ | [analysis.py](analysis.py) | `MeasCdac`; `analyze_cdac_settling`: per stage and direction, the differential top plate's step, the driver's delay from the state's edge to its main bottom plate's 50 % (`calc.delay`), and the settling time from that edge until the step stays within half the converter's LSB, 2 · full scale / 2^adc_bits (`calc.settlingTime`). The behavioral time constant of a stage is (settling − delay) / ln(\|step\| / ½ LSB) in its slower direction: started at its driver's delay, its exponential reaches half an LSB when SPICE does. |
| Analysis tests | ✓ | [test_analysis.py](test_analysis.py) | Synthetic RC steps of known delay, time constant and amplitude: the steps, delays, settling and time constants recovered; an unsettled window detected. |
| Sweep | ✓ | [sweep.py](sweep.py) | The array of every converter of `flow.adc.sweep`, each at its converter's resolution: every ADC array the design space uses (`flow.caparray.layout.ADC_ARRAYS`, sized through its extraction in TSMC65: binary and the three schemes with 2, 4 and 6 extra cycles at 10, 12 and 14 bits, at one and two of the resolution's least switched capacitance, and FRIDA-1's array) at `n_dac + 1`, its drivers derived from its plates; 61 instances, each its parameters (2026-10-24, run 20261024_142328: all 61 on 16 Spectre runs, a few minutes each at 10 and 12 bits, 20 to 45 minutes at 14 bits x2; the derived drives are 1–2 at 10 bits, up to 4 (x1) and 8 (x2) at 12, and for the 14-bit MSB 64 (x1) and 128 (x2), four and eight buffers of 16; every stage of every array settles to half an LSB in 0.06–0.75 ns, the slowest stage of an array in 0.38–0.75 ns, against 0.1 ns to 23 ns with the fixed drives before); metrics: the slowest and the MSB's settling, the largest delay and time constant, the full scale, the LSB, the steps' error against the capacitances (the simulator's precision); details: settling by stage, and the model's delay and time constant by stage. |
| Behavioral model | ✓ | [model.py](model.py), [test_model.py](test_model.py) | `CdacModel.of(pdk, AdcParams)` or `(pdk, CapArrayConfig, adc_bits)`: per stage its settling, its driver's delay and its time constant, from the sweep's newest run or `build/models/cdac/<pdk>/<digest>`, simulated on first use (`simulate=True`). `flow.adc.model.AdcModel` settles each stage by it (`stage_tau_s`, `stage_delay_s`; `tau_scale` scales the time constants, not the delays); it only looks the characterization up (`simulate=False`, without waiting on any lock) and falls back to the drivers' RC estimate, with a warning, where there is none, so run the targets below first. |
| Targets | ✓ | [targets.py](targets.py) | `python -m flow cdac.characterization`: the sweep over every ADC instance's array in TSMC65, 16 Spectre runs at a time, tabulated. |
| Layout | ✗ | - | Not a layout block: the array is laid out and extracted by flow/caparray, the drivers by flow/capdriver. |
