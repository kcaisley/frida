# Status

The CDAC is the capacitor array with its drivers: the array's capacitors cannot be characterized for speed without the drivers that switch them. [flow/caparray](../caparray/readme.md) stays the array's generator (weights, layouts, extractions) and [flow/capdriver](../capdriver/readme.md) the drivers' (cells, strengths); this block puts them together and measures how each stage settles.

| Feature | Status | File | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Netlist generator | ✓ | [subckt.py](subckt.py) | `Cdac(CdacParams)`: both sides, each with a main and a difference `CapDriver` of the array's stages (wired as `flow/adc/subckt.py` wires them: the main drivers' invert input `dac_invert`, the difference drivers' `dac_diffcaps`), its plates ideal capacitors from each bottom plate to the top plate, and the top plate's other load to ground. `cdac_params(pdk, CapArrayConfig)` takes the plates from `CapArrayModel.of`: an ADC variant's 3D extraction, or an ideal array's nominal values (`parasitic_cap` set, as flow/adc14's). The drivers are the generic cells the ADC model stands in with (`Cells.GENERIC`), at the array's `driver_strengths`. |
| Netlist tests | ✓ | [test_subckt.py](test_subckt.py) | The drivers' and plates' wiring, missing difference plates, refused capacitances; the testbench's timing and saved nets. |
| Testbench generator | ✓ | [sim.py](sim.py) | Every stage switches alone in its turn, as the SAR loop switches it: its P side's state rises and its N side's falls at the start of its window, and back at the start of the next, every other stage held; the top plates bled to mid-supply through 1 TΩ. The window is twice the slowest stage's estimated settling (from the drivers' characterization, `flow.capdriver.model`) and is doubled until every step settles in its first half. Spectre at a fixed step of a 2000th of the window and reltol 1e-4: tighter tolerances (1e-5, 1e-6 conservative) move no settling time by more than 1 ps in a 10- and a 14-bit array, and the steps agree with the capacitances' within 0.06 LSB (0.03 in the ADC variants). |
| Analysis | ✓ | [analysis.py](analysis.py) | `MeasCdac`; `analyze_cdac_settling`: per stage and direction, the differential top plate's step, the driver's delay from the state's edge to its main bottom plate's 50 % (`calc.delay`), and the settling time from that edge until the step stays within half the converter's LSB, 2 · full scale / 2^adc_bits (`calc.settlingTime`). The behavioral time constant of a stage is (settling − delay) / ln(\|step\| / ½ LSB) in its slower direction: started at its driver's delay, its exponential reaches half an LSB when SPICE does. |
| Analysis tests | ✓ | [test_analysis.py](test_analysis.py) | Synthetic RC steps of known delay, time constant and amplitude: the steps, delays, settling and time constants recovered; an unsettled window detected. |
| Sweep | ✓ | [sweep.py](sweep.py) | Every ADC variant's array (`flow.caparray.layout.ADC_VARIANTS`, 155, extracted in TSMC65), named as `flow.adc.sweep` names them (a topology per resolution, 10–14 bits; a sizing per redundancy: `plus0`, `plus<n>` the overlay, `plus<n>_start`, `_spread`, `_end`, `_both`), at its converter's resolution (`n_dac + 1`); metrics: the slowest and the MSB's settling, the largest delay and time constant, the full scale, the LSB, the steps' error against the capacitances (the simulator's precision); details: settling by stage, and the model's delay and time constant by stage. |
| Behavioral model | ✓ | [model.py](model.py), [test_model.py](test_model.py) | `CdacModel.of(pdk, AdcParams)` or `(pdk, CapArrayConfig, adc_bits)`: per stage its settling, its driver's delay and its time constant, from the sweep's newest run or `build/models/cdac/<pdk>/<digest>`, simulated on first use (`simulate=True`). `flow.adc.model.AdcModel` settles each stage by it (`stage_tau_s`, `stage_delay_s`; `tau_scale` scales the time constants, not the delays); it only looks the characterization up (`simulate=False`, without waiting on any lock) and falls back to the drivers' RC estimate, with a warning, where there is none, so run the targets below first. |
| Targets | ✓ | [targets.py](targets.py) | `python -m flow cdac.characterization`: the sweep over the ADC variants in TSMC65, 16 Spectre runs at a time, tabulated. `python -m flow cdac.characterization_adc14`: flow/adc14's 16 ideal arrays into the models' cache. |
| Layout | ✗ | - | Not a layout block: the array is laid out and extracted by flow/caparray, the drivers by flow/capdriver. |
