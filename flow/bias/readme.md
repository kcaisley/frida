| Simulation runners | ✓ | [sim.py](sim.py) | `bias_characterization`: every case over reference currents of 1–50 µA at the core supply and supplies of 75–108 % at 10 µA, in each PDK (22 runs per PDK, all 132 in about a minute on 16 workers); `check=True` runs Spectre's static checks at the nominal point. |
| Analysis | ✓ | [analysis.py](analysis.py) | `MeasBias`; `analyze_bias_mirror` (each test mirror's current at mid-supply over the reference, output resistance there from `deriv`, compliance where its current stays within 5 % (`calc.compliance_range`, shared with `flow.idac`), the mirror devices' drain-source voltages, the four gates, the supply current). Run 20261019_022652, at 10 µA: both mirrors within 8 % of the reference in every PDK (`margin` within 6 %), NMOS compliance 0.17–0.49 V above ground and PMOS 0.21–0.49 V below the supply, the generator drawing 2.3–3.2 times the reference. At 1 µA, in weak inversion, the quarter-strength diodes leave the mirrors off by up to a third (LF150's PMOS mirror; in IHP130 the NMOS mirror device has no drain-source voltage left and the start-up follower stays on); the sixteenth brings TSMC65 and TPSC65 within 8 % but leaves IHP130 and LF150 up to 50 % off; at 50 µA TSMC28's PMOS mirror runs out of headroom at 0.9 V (0.64). Over the supply (75–108 % at 10 µA) the NMOS mirror moves by at most 0.03, 0.10 in IHP130's `wide` case; the PMOS mirror by up to 0.25 in TSMC28, which at 75 % of 0.9 V runs out of headroom (0.74), and 0.19 in IHP130's `wide` case. Uncascoded copies were off by up to three times at 1 µA (barrier lowering), and once cascoded the DC solution without start-up could sit dead (no current, `vbn` far above the supply) in IHP130 and LF150. |
