## What it measures

- A ring oscillator, started by its enable alone: its oscillation frequency f0 = 1/T0 of the steady oscillation ([Razavi-PLL] Sec. 3.1.1, p. 64), timed over its last ten whole cycles; its supply current I_DD over those cycles ([Razavi-PLL] Example 3.5, p. 70), the average power V_DD I_DD and the energy per cycle P/f0 ([Razavi-PLL] Sec. 3.3.2, p. 74); its output swing, peak to peak, the overshoot past each rail included ([Razavi-Analog] Sec. 9.1.1, p. 348; [Razavi-PLL] Sec. 1.4.1, p. 12), and duty cycle ([Razavi-PLL] Sec. 11.10, p. 365); its mean stage delay T_D = 1/(2 N f0) ([Razavi-PLL] Sec. 3.3.2, p. 74; for a differential ring, N its stages per chain, [Razavi-Analog] Sec. 15.4.1, p. 633); with a control, K_VCO, the slope of f0 over V_cont ([Razavi-PLL] Sec. 1.6, pp. 27-28). A ring that stops after its enable reads stopped, and one too slow for two whole cycles in its run reads so, both without numbers.
- A differential chain is measured on its p chain alone: its delays, transitions, swing and duty cycle are `outp`'s (from `inp`), single-ended at mid-supply.
- A delay line, driven through two of its own stages: its propagation delays t_PLH and t_PHL between the 50 % points of its input and output, named by the output's edge, and its output transitions t_LH and t_HL from 10 to 90 % ([Baker] Sec. 10.1.3, Fig. 10.9, p. 333); its supply current over two input periods, the power and energy per input cycle at the bench's 4 ns clock ([Baker] Eqs. (11.14), (11.15), p. 355); its mean stage delay (t_PLH + t_PHL)/(2 N), a string's t_PHL + t_PLH being the sum of its stages' ([Baker] Eq. (11.22), p. 360); with a control, K_DL as the slope of its mean delay over V_cont, in s/V ([Razavi-PLL] Sec. 11.1, p. 346).
- The stage delay is a mean over unequal stages: a ring's first stage is a NAND with the enable, and each output drives two more stages (a ring's output its first stage too). Every run is at the typical corner and 25 C; the supply sweep's worst case is at that corner. The power is the chain's own supply's: the bias generation is not counted, nor is a delay line's first input charge.

## How to run

```sh
uv run python -m flow --block delay --run ring5_starved --to results     # characterize one named chain
uv run python -m flow --block delay --run sizes --to results             # every chain at every size
uv run python -m flow --block delay --run chain4 --to lvs                # one chain by ALIGN, without extraction
uv run python -m flow --block delay --run sizes --to results --dry-run   # what would run
```

## Design space

- `ring5`: five-stage inverter ring oscillator, enabled.
- `ring5_starved`: current-starved five-stage ring VCO.
- `ring4_coupled`: four-stage pseudo-differential coupled ring, with its injection device.
- `ring4_tail`: four-stage coupled ring on a shared tail.
- `chain4`: four-stage inverter delay line.
- `chain2_coupled`: two-stage coupled differential buffer.
- `chain4_starved`: four-stage current-starved delay line.
- `sizes`: each named chain at every size it has devices for, by the chain's name and the size's: every width scaled together to half, as it is, to twice and to four times (`sizes(ring5_half)`, `_nominal`, `_double`, `_quadruple`); the coupling inverters alone doubled (`_coupling`, coupled stages only); the starving devices and the tail doubled (`_control`, controlled chains only); the stage inverters at twice their length (`_long`).
