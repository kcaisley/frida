| Design space | ✓ | [sweep.py](sweep.py) | `python -m flow comp.sweep`: the six topologies at ten sizes (seven around the short-channel base, three around FRIDA-1's long-channel comparator) in every PDK. Each at the nominal common mode: the delay and energy at 0.5 mV and 100 mV, the delay over 0.1–100 mV (its metastability and regeneration time constant), the offset and noise from a transient-noise S-curve (±2 mV, 40 decisions a point); the S-curve again over the common mode, the delay and energy over the supply. Clock: 1 ns reset and 6 ns evaluation, both doubled (to at most 8 and 48 ns) until the clock no longer cuts the latch short: its noise-free metastability decisions (each input after its negative, so that a reset which leaves the last decision shows) all resolve, the next clock changes none of them, nor the delay at any positive input or at 10 µV (the 0.1 mV delay extrapolated by the regeneration time constant) by more than 2 %, the latch resolves those 10 µV within the evaluation, and the nominal S-curve leaves at most 1 % of its decisions unresolved. The reset grows with the evaluation, since a latch slow to regenerate is slow to reset (after 12 or 24 ns of evaluation, 1 ns of reset leaves a FRIDA-1-like latch holding its last decision, and 2 ns still leaves some deciding against the input). Every run of the instance takes that clock (`evaluation_time_s`); a decision unresolved is timed nowhere, and the S-curve shows P(high) of the resolved decisions (the rest: `unresolved_fraction`). |
| Analysis | ✓ | [analysis.py](analysis.py) | `analyze_comp_timing` (clock-to-decision delay, settling, unresolved trials), `analyze_comp_delay` (the delay over common mode and input), `analyze_comp_power` (power, energy per decision), `analyze_comp_candidate` (a sweep candidate's summary). `MeasComp` and the offset, noise and common-mode analyses lab captures share stay in `flow/analysis`.
