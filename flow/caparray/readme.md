| Netlist generator | ✓ | [subckt.py](subckt.py) | Passive main/diff array; its leaf is the ideal FMOM view (`flow/momcap/subckt.py`). An array is its DAC resolution `n_dac` (binary steps, total weight 2^n_dac − 1 units), its extra conversion cycles `n_extra` (0: binary), the scheme that spends them (`RedunStrat`) and its capacitance `cap_scale`. Every scheme keeps the binary total: `MSB_SPLIT` splits the n_extra most significant binary weights into halves, `LSB_SPLIT` the n_extra least significant weights of at least 2 (the LSB stays one unit), `MSB_REDIST` is FRIDA's paired overlay (FRIDA-1's array: 5 at n_dac = 11). Each array's weights fall stage by stage and no stage's tolerance (`tolerance_units`) is negative, so every code is reached. `total_capacitance_f(bits, FS, cap_scale)`: the top plate a side at which both sides' kT/C is 1/cap_scale of the quantization noise at a full scale ±FS, cap_scale 6 kT 4^N / FS²; `switched_capacitance_f(config, FS, reference)` the switched share FS / reference of it, `unit_capacitance_f` that over the total weight (by default an ideal array at 1 V: 26 fF at 10 bits x1, 6.7 pF at 14; the ADC arrays are sized at their own extracted full scale, `sizing.py`). Every chunk is a main and a difference finger about the coarse weight, or, with `CapArrayParams.unary_rows` (the ADC arrays), a chunk of the coarse weight one main finger of that many units. `weights` and `driver_strengths` are FRIDA-1's fabricated arrays' alone (lab scans, FRIDA-1's layout checks, decoding archived captures; the radix-2-on-coarse-units array's total is not binary's): the design space never sets them. |

| Layout generator | ✓ | [layout.py](layout.py), [`stems/capcell.py`](../layout/stems/capcell.py), [`align/pdk/mom.py`](../layout/align/pdk/mom.py) | Topology here (weight family, stack, unit order); the geometry is the PDK's rules' (`difference_geometry`, `caparray_plan`), drawn by ALIGN's generators on cell_fabric grids (`DifferenceUnit` per unit, `ArrayWiring` for the top), the units placed in the top as cells. Every unit is a row of the same length, Harpe's unit-length array (JSSC 2018): a chunk of weight w lengthens its main finger and shortens its difference finger by w weight steps, so its capacitance is the two strips' difference, edges cancelled; a chunk of the coarse weight is a unary element and a lighter one a length-scaled binary element, so `coarse_weight` = 2^N_b is the split between N_b binary bits and the unary rest. Family options for the ADC arrays: `unary_length` draws a unary row as one main finger (the coarse weight in units, its length corrected through the extraction) and no difference finger, bus or access, so a unary row loads the top plate with what it switches alone; `tied_dummies` joins the edge dummies' fingers to the ring (the top plate), which an extraction otherwise lumps into ground; a shield tap that would meet the top plate's stack (a short row's top bridge) is left out. The production arrays step two tracks, keep their unary rows' difference fingers and floating dummies; `flow.caparray.sizing` sets the ADC arrays' split and step. A unit stacks its fingers on the PDK's top three MOM metals by default (`STACKED_METALS`: each stacked metal adds the same capacitance per length on the same footprint, so three give the densest array and the shortest rows); `outline_um` is an array's boundary. Draws in all six PDKs; the six reference arrays per PDK are mask-identical to the stems drawing ALIGN's generators replaced (IHP130's labels on its metal text layer, where ALIGN writes them). |

The planned `convert_netlist_caparray_to_measurement()` adapter consumes parasitic extraction results for design-versus-extracted capacitance, with later support for Monte Carlo capacitance variation. It currently raises `NotImplementedError`; it is not a transient waveform path.

Its prototype takes `netlist_path`, `CapArrayParams`, `net_names` and `device_names` (raw netlist identifiers → analysis aliases), and returns `MeasCapArray`. That result type reserves the run information and parameters; its capacitance payload and HDF5 registration remain to be defined.
